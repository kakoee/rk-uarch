## Measurement conventions and limits

Intervals are half-open. Window inventory is immediately before events at the start;
arrivals/departures at start are included, at end excluded. Queue area clips initial and
terminal members separately for waiting/service/inflight. Full-run conservation includes
completion at the final timestamp by closing the reporting window at duration+1 ps.
No stationary Little's-law assertion follows from a finite window. Zero-flow residence
remains null; stationary ≤1% and saturated/latency-stream ±5% tests need their independently
qualified populations/windows and later actual observations.

Useful and issued operations, SRAM operand accesses, HBM payload and rounded transactions
remain separate. C01's useful-work floor is 16000 ps, compared with its resident 702000 ps
expectation; it is necessary, not sufficient, and not a C2/full-model roofline claim.
Component u_c0_duration_ps remains null. No NoC0 contention, physical DRAM queues, energy,
SRAM bank stalls or causal physical explanation is claimed.

The observation naming contract supplied at first use must freeze exact owner-category
sets, transfer boundary names, allocation owner IDs, unrepresented paths and additional
literal selectors. It may not be generated from the result under test. Service queue
windows and DMA inflight intervals are mandatory. The future A join must resolve whether
alignment/latency spans are resource intervals or require distinct causal witnesses;
unresolved fields cannot be silently accepted as empty. Tests diagnose schema-valid results
in addition to carrier validation; a parser refusal is distinct from a numerical discrepancy.
